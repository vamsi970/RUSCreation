Who We Are

Crafting Comfort Since Day One

We are a dedicated home textile manufacturer with over 15 years of experience in designing, producing, and delivering premium quality pillows, cushion covers, kitchen linens, and a wide range of home accessories to clients across the globe.

Our philosophy is simple: every home deserves beautiful, well-made textiles. From the softness of our cushion fabrics to the precise stitching on our kitchen linens, quality is never compromised.

We cater to retailers, interior designers, hospitality groups, and individual buyers — offering custom designs, private labelling, and flexible order quantities to suit every need.

Est. 2014
